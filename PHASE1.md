**Chakravyuh — Urban Flood Intelligence & Safe Routing**

## Submission description

Chakravyuh is an AI-assisted urban flood intelligence and decision-support application built around a simple problem: when a city is flooded, the information needed to make good decisions is often scattered across different sources and constantly changing.

The system brings together flood-related information and uses AI to help users understand the situation, identify relevant risks, and support safer decisions and navigation. A key part of the design is that the AI is not treated as an unquestionable answer engine — the system keeps track of evidence, uncertainty, and provenance so that users can understand not just what the system is saying, but why.

The goal is to make AI genuinely useful during a difficult real-world situation rather than building another generic chatbot.
